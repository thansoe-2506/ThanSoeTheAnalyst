About

I'm a data analyst with a computer-science background and four-plus years working across analytics, business intelligence and geospatial data. I like the whole pipeline: getting data out of messy sources, modelling it so it can be trusted, and presenting it so a decision becomes obvious.

At z-waka I analyse platform, engagement, survey and commercial data, build extraction workflows across PostgreSQL, MongoDB and Trino, and ship dashboards in Power BI, Apache Superset and Looker Studio. Before that I built the KPI/OKR system for Marathon Myanmar, and spent three years in Map Operations at Grab, where data quality had a direct effect on whether riders and drivers found each other.

I'm completing a B.Sc. in Computer Science at University of the People, and I'm interested in roles where analytics shapes strategy — not just reports on it.
